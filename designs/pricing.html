Common questions

Why do listings and developments share one credit pool?
Because forcing you to split your allowance between two buckets is bad pricing — especially when most agents specialise in one or the other. A listing is 1 credit. A development is 1 credit. Whether you sell only secondary, only off-plan, or a mix — every credit you pay for is one you can actually use.
Why are Teasers free until I upgrade?
A Teaser is a lead-capture page with minimal content — no brochure, no floor plans, no payment plan. It costs us less to host and serve. We'd rather you launch teasers the day a developer announces anything, capture early interest, and pay us when you actually have something to sell. Every Teaser auto-archives after 12 months of inactivity to keep your workspace clean.
What happens when I hit my credit limit?
We'll show you a clear upgrade prompt before you hit the cap, and you can upgrade to the next tier instantly with proration — only paying the difference for the remainder of the month. No surprise overflow charges, no hidden meters. Just simple tier upgrades when you outgrow your plan.
Can I switch between tiers?
Yes, any time. Upgrading is instant. Downgrading takes effect at the next billing cycle, and we'll warn you if downgrading would put you over the new tier's limits so you can archive items first.
What's included in the custom domain on Active?
Buy a new domain through us (~AED 44/year for .com, charged at-cost via Cloudflare Registrar) or point an existing one. Auto-configured DNS, automatic SSL, automatic SEO migration — your portfolio goes live on yourname.com in about 60 seconds. The domain is registered in your name, so if you ever leave, you take it with you.
Do you offer team / brokerage plans?
Yes — talk to us. Brokerage plans cover multiple agents under one billing relationship, with shared developer pages, centralised analytics, and optional white-label. Pricing is custom based on team size.
Annual billing — what's the actual savings?
20% off. Solo annual = $279 (vs $348 monthly). Active annual = $759 (vs $948 monthly). Studio annual = $1,429 (vs $1,788 monthly).
