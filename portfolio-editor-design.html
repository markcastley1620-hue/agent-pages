I came to Dubai in 2011, when the market was still finding its feet. Over the last decade I've represented buyers and sellers across Downtown Dubai, the Palm, Emirates Hills, and Dubai Hills Estate.

My clients tend to be investors, end-users relocating from London or Singapore, and Dubai-based families upsizing into their long-term homes. Most are introduced through people I've already worked with — which I take as the highest form of compliment.

I work with a small number of clients at any given time. That's deliberate. It's how I'm able to know every building, every floor plan, every transfer history.
